Every device. One hub.

Purple Hub is our platform for connected devices. Smart lights, robots, cameras, sensors and custom hardware are all managed from one app on your phone.

It runs entirely on your own network. Your devices and your data stay in your home, with no cloud account needed.

  • Automatic discovery: devices on your network are found and paired for you
  • Smart lighting: switch, dim and colour your lights in real time
  • Robot control: live camera, 3D depth, LiDAR maps and tap-to-go navigation
  • Built-in AI assistant: ask Purple to control your devices
  • Ready-to-flash firmware so ESP32 and Arduino projects join the hub

A robot that sees, maps and finds its own way

Our home robot is built on a hoverboard drive base with a Raspberry Pi, an Intel RealSense depth camera and a 360° LiDAR. You control it from Purple Hub.

It is designed to look after a home on its own. It can drive to a room, follow a person or repeat a patrol route. When something is in the way it reverses, turns and tries another path instead of giving up.

  • Tap-to-go: tap a spot on the map and the robot drives there
  • Person following and route record and repeat
  • Obstacle recovery that keeps trying until it arrives
  • Live camera, depth view and LiDAR mapping on your phone
  • Switches the house lights on when it drives in the dark
