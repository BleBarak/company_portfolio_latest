Robotics

Robots that see, map and navigate

We design and build our own robots in Lusaka. Our current rover runs on a Raspberry Pi with an Intel RealSense depth camera, a 360° LiDAR and hoverboard motors, and it is controlled from Purple Hub on a phone.

  • Autonomous navigation: tap a spot on the map and the robot drives there by itself
  • Person following, route recording and repeat, and home patrol
  • Obstacle recovery: when the path is blocked it reverses, turns and finds another way
  • Live camera, 3D depth view and LiDAR mapping from your phone
  • Works with your smart home, for example switching lights on when it drives in the dark

We also build custom robots and prototypes for schools, research projects and businesses. Tell us what you want the robot to do and we will plan the build with you.

Smart Home Integration

A smarter home, set up for you

We plan, install and set up smart homes so that everything works together and is easy to use: lights, speakers, displays, cameras and plugs.

  • Smart lighting: colour bulbs you can switch, dim and schedule
  • Google Nest and Amazon Alexa speakers and displays, set up for voice control
  • Security cameras, smart plugs and automations
  • Purple Hub: control your devices from one app, on your own network
  • A walkthrough for the whole household once everything is installed

We can start with a single room or do the whole house, using devices you already own or ones we supply.
