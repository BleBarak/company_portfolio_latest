Meet Purple, an AI companion that runs offline

  • Robo City Tech Team
  • 8 Nov 2026
  • 4 min read

Most AI assistants live in a data centre. Everything you say is sent over the internet, and they stop working when the connection drops, which happens a lot. We wanted a companion that lives with you. So we built Purple.

A brain on your phone

Purple runs a compact open language model directly on the phone. Speech recognition and Purple's voice also run on the device. Nothing has to leave your phone, so Purple works offline, costs nothing per message and keeps your conversations private. Purple can also remember things you tell it, so conversations build up over time.

A face that feels alive

Purple is not a chat box. It has an animated face with expressive eyes, designed by our team with more than 150 expressions. It looks happy when you greet it, curious when you ask a question and sleepy late at night. It has moods and a personality you can choose, and it reacts when you shake the phone.

We wanted Purple to feel less like software and more like a small friend that happens to live in your phone.

Friends and wheels

  • Friends: when two phones running Purple are near each other, the Purples greet each other and play games together.
  • Smart home: together with Purple Hub, Purple can switch your lights and control your devices when you ask.
  • Wheels: mount the phone on a small robot car and Purple can drive around and follow you.

Made in Zambia

Purple is designed and built by the Robo City team in Lusaka, and it is in active development. If you would like to try it, partner with us or bring Purple to your school, reach out.
