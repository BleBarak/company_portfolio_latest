Purple Hub: every device, one hub

  • Robo City Tech Team
  • 8 Nov 2026
  • 5 min read

At Robo City we build a lot of connected things: robots, ESP32 cameras, GPS trackers, smart lights and custom IoT boards. Each one used to come with its own web page on its own network address. To drive the robot you typed one address, to watch a camera another, and the lights had yet another app. It worked, but it was not something we could hand to a customer.

Purple Hub is our answer: one app for every connected device in your home or lab.

What it does

  • Finds your devices for you. Purple Hub discovers devices on your network automatically and finds them again if their address changes. Nobody has to type IP addresses.
  • Smart lighting. Switch, dim and colour your smart bulbs in real time, straight over your own network.
  • Robots. Live camera, 3D depth view and LiDAR maps, tap-to-go navigation, person following and patrol routes.
  • Cameras and sensors. Our ESP32 cameras and trackers appear in the same app.
  • Built-in AI assistant. Ask Purple to switch on the lab light, and it does.
  • Ready-to-flash firmware for ESP32 and Arduino, so your own projects can join the hub.

Private by design

Many smart home products send every switch and every camera frame through a company's cloud. Purple Hub talks to your devices directly on your local network. Your home keeps working when the internet is down, and your data stays in your home.

Our goal: managing everything you have connected should be as simple as one app, with no cloud account to make and no addresses to remember.

A platform for learners too

Purple Hub is also how students in our embedded systems training bring their projects to life. Flash a board, and it shows up in the app next to real robots and smart lights.

Purple Hub is in active development. If you want it for your home, school or business, or want to build on it, talk to us.
