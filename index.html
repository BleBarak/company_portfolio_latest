Purple Hub

Our flagship platform: every device, one hub. Smart home, robotics and IoT, managed from a single app.

All your devices, under one roof

Purple Hub brings smart lighting, robots, cameras, sensors and custom hardware together in one app. It runs entirely on your own network, so your devices and your data stay with you.

  • Private by design: no cloud dependency, no accounts.
  • Effortless pairing: devices on your network are found automatically.
  • Smart home control: switch, dim and colour your lights in real time.
  • Built-in AI assistant: ask Purple to control your devices.
  • Ready-to-flash firmware for ESP32 and Arduino projects.
Get Purple Hub

Robots that see, map and navigate

Purple Hub is built for robotics. See through your robot's camera with live 3D depth, map your rooms with LiDAR, and let it drive itself: when the way is blocked, it recovers and reroutes on its own.

  • Live vision and 3D depth.
  • LiDAR mapping and tap-on-the-map navigation.
  • Touch or game-controller driving.
